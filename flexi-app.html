Install App on Device

This support portal is engineered exclusively as an installed standalone POS app. Install it directly onto your terminal register, tablet, or phone to sign in and submit tickets.

📱 On Android POS / Sunmi / Chrome:
Tap "Install Support App Now" above, or tap the browser menu (⋮) and select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
🍎 On Apple iPad / iPhone (Safari):
1. Tap the Share button (⎋) in the Safari toolbar.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (⊞).
3. Open the FlexiTap Support icon from your home screen.
💻 On Windows Counter Register (Edge / Chrome):
Click the Install icon in the address bar (or menu ⋮ → Apps → Install).

Operations & Diagnostic Terms

Diagnostic Telemetry & Access Authorization By submitting a ticket and initiating diagnostic troubleshooting, you authorize FlexiTap Operations to inspect POS telemetry, SQLite synchronization health, device platform versions, and hardware peripherals (thermal printer cutter, cash drawer, and Bluetooth sockets) solely for incident remediation. 2.
