Install App on Device

This support portal is engineered exclusively as an installed standalone POS app. Install it directly onto your terminal register, tablet, or phone to sign in and submit tickets.

📱 On Android POS / Sunmi / Chrome:
Tap "Install Support App Now" above, or tap the browser menu (⋮) and select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
🍎 On Apple iPad / iPhone (Safari):
1. Tap the Share button (⎋) in the Safari toolbar.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (⊞).
3. Open the FlexiTap Support icon from your home screen.
💻 On Windows Counter Register (Edge / Chrome):
Click the Install icon in the address bar (or menu ⋮ → Apps → Install).

FlexiTap Support Diagnostic Service Terms

Operations & Diagnostic Terms

Please confirm authorization for terminal diagnostics and telemetry.

1. Diagnostic Telemetry & Access Authorization By submitting a ticket and initiating diagnostic troubleshooting, you authorize FlexiTap Operations to inspect POS telemetry, SQLite synchronization health, device platform versions, and hardware peripherals (thermal printer cutter, cash drawer, and Bluetooth sockets) solely for incident remediation. 2. Data Privacy & End-to-End Encryption FlexiTap is committed to absolute merchant data confidentiality. Live Remote Assistance Consent If you request a live remote assistance session, screen sharing and terminal diagnostics are conducted over secure WebRTC sessions. Please ensure no customer payment card PANs or CVVs are visible during video inspection. 4. Automated Incident Routing & Remediation Support tickets are automatically logged and assigned to Customer Support Desk specialists for immediate assistance and triage.
