Partner Program Agreement & Anti-Fraud Policy

Effective: October 2026 • Philippine Commercial SaaS Terms
⚠️ SECTION 1: STRICT ANTI-SELF-REFERRAL & NON-ARBITRAGE RULE
Affiliates and partners are strictly prohibited from earning commissions or bounties on software accounts created for themselves, their own food service establishments, or businesses in which they hold a direct or beneficial ownership stake. Using your own referral link to purchase subscriptions with your own money in order to collect the ₱3,000 or ₱6,000 bounty constitutes affiliate fraud. Any detected self-referrals will be summarily cancelled with forfeit of pending balances.
🏪 SECTION 2: STORE ACTIVATION & COMMERCIAL USAGE GATE
To qualify for bounty disbursement, referred stores must be genuine, operating commercial enterprises. A referral bounty is released from hold only after the referred store logs a minimum of 25 completed POS checkout transactions across at least 3 distinct register shifts. Inactive "ghost stores" or dummy accounts created solely to collect bounties will be permanently disqualified.
🔍 SECTION 3: AUTOMATED PAYMENT & DEVICE CROSS-MATCHING
FlexiTap employs automated risk detection algorithms. If a referred store's subscription payment or billing profile matches the same credit card, GCash mobile number, Maya wallet, tax ID, device hardware fingerprint, or IP subnet as the referring partner, the referral will be automatically flagged for administrative fraud review. Self-funded merchant signups will not be remunerated under the retail partner program.
⏳ SECTION 4: 30-DAY ANTI-REFUND & DISPUTE HOLD

All commissions are placed in Pending Hold for thirty (30) calendar days from the date of the merchant's initial subscription payment. If the merchant requests a refund under our 7-Day Live Shift Money-Back Guarantee, cancels their service, or disputes the charge within this window, the commission is immediately revoked.

💸 SECTION 5: CHARGEBACK CLAWBACK & DISPUTE INDEMNITY

If an affiliate receives a bounty disbursement and the referred store subsequently initiates a bank chargeback or fraud dispute within 120 days, FlexiTap reserves the right to deduct the disbursed amount from current or future earnings, or initiate formal legal recovery under Philippine civil commerce regulations for fraudulent collusion.

💼 SECTION 6: SOLUTION PARTNER / RESELLER PROGRAM EXCLUSION
IT freelancers, system integrators, and POS hardware vendors who purchase subscriptions on behalf of clients using their own capital must apply for the FlexiTap Solution Partner / Authorized Reseller tier. Resellers receive wholesale margins and hardware bundling rights, but may not claim retail affiliate bounties on client payments made through their own accounts.
