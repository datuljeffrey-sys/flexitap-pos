FlexiTap POS Is Officially Live: Free Offline-First Register for Windows, Android & Apple

By FlexiTap Engineering & Merchant Operations • November 21, 2026 • 4 min read

Following months of intensive stress testing across specialty coffee bars, multi-SKU retail boutiques, quick-service kitchens, and service studios, FlexiTap POS is officially live in production.

Our mission from day one has been straightforward: give independent merchants enterprise-grade, sub-15ms offline checkout reliability without predatory monthly per-terminal leases or forced payment processing contracts.

What's Included in the Production Release

  • Native Windows (.exe) & Android / Sunmi (.apk) Installers: Available immediately in our Download Center, with our native Apple App Store (iPadOS & iOS) release arriving next.
  • 5 Purpose-Built Industry Modes: Switch seamlessly between Retail Barcode & Variant Matrices, Coffee Shop & Bakery Modifiers, Quick-Service & KDS, Fine Dining Floor Plans & Course Firing, and Salon & Appointment Timelines.
  • 1-Click CSV Migration: Moving from legacy tablet software? Import your entire SKU and modifier catalog in 60 seconds with our Loyverse CSV migration tool.
