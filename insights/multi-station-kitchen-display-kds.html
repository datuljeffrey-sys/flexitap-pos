Multi-Station Kitchen Display (KDS) Routing for Fast-Casual & Full-Service Kitchens

By FlexiTap Engineering & Merchant Operations • September 28, 2026 • 5 min read

During a 120-ticket lunch rush, paper chits get splashed with fryer oil, slide off metal ticket rails, or print out of sequence when a thermal roll runs dry mid-order. Yet many independent quick-service restaurants, food trucks, and cafes hesitate to upgrade to digital Kitchen Display Systems (KDS) because legacy vendors charge $50 to $95 per month per kitchen screen on top of proprietary Android hardware markups.

FlexiTap POS includes native, local-LAN Multi-Station KDS & Order Status Display (OSD) workflows engineered to run on standard off-the-shelf tablets, touch monitors, and Android TVs.

Intelligent Item-Level Station Splitting

When a cashier or tableside server rings a mixed ticket—such as two smashburgers, loaded fries, and an iced matcha latte—your prep team shouldn't have to squint at irrelevant items across stations. FlexiTap routes items automatically by category or prep station:

  • Grill & Fry Line KDS: Displays only hot food items, allergy callouts, and cook temperatures.
  • Barista / Beverage Screen: Streams espresso, tea, and bar drinks immediately upon ring-up so drinks are ready before trays hit the pass.
  • Expo & Pickup Status Board (OSD): Automatically transitions ticket numbers from Preparing to Ready for Pickup on a customer-facing screen the instant the kitchen bumps the order.
"Color-escalating ticket timers—Green (0–3m), Amber (4–7m), and Alert Red (8m+)—give kitchen leads instant visual situational awareness across the entire line without touching a single slip of paper."

100% Local Wi-Fi Broadcast (Zero Cloud KDS Lag)

In cloud-routed KDS apps, an order tapped at the front counter travels to a remote cloud server and webhooks back down to the kitchen tablet—meaning any internet hiccup blinds your cooks instantly. In FlexiTap's Quick-Service POS and Fine Dining course-firing mode, orders broadcast directly across your local Wi-Fi/Ethernet LAN in under 50 milliseconds.

Hybrid KDS + Printed Runner Tickets

Prefer digital screens on the cook line plus a sticky printed label for takeaway bags or delivery cups? FlexiTap lets you pair KDS touch screens alongside ESC/POS thermal printers simultaneously—with zero per-screen lease fees on FlexiTap Pro.
