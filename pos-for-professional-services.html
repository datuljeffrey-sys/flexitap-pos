Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Salon Owners, Barbers & Service Providers

Everything you need to know about implementing FlexiTap in your business.

Do you take a percentage or commission on our client bookings? +
Never! Unlike platforms like Fresha that charge up to 20% commission on clients who book online, FlexiTap takes 0% commission. You keep 100% of your service revenue.
How does FlexiTap calculate staff commissions on services and retail? +
You can assign customized commission rates for each service provider (e.g. 50% on haircuts, 60% on color services, and 10% on retail product sales). At the end of every day or week, FlexiTap generates automated payroll reports showing exact commissions, service totals, and tips.
Can we keep client hair color formulas and allergy records offline? +
Yes! Client profiles include private notes, past visit histories, and technical formula records (e.g. 40g 6N + 20g 20-vol developer). Because FlexiTap uses encrypted local SQLite, your client records remain safe and accessible even during total internet blackouts.
Is the Free Community edition really free for my salon? +
Yes! Our Free Community edition is completely free with zero monthly fees, zero expiration dates, and zero transaction fee markups from FlexiTap. It supports unlimited sales, appointment scheduling, and client management on a single reception register.
