Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hardware, offline mode, and subscriptions.

Can I bring my own tablet and thermal receipt printers?+
Yes! FlexiTap is 100% Bring-Your-Own-Device (BYOD). It runs on standard Android tablets, supporting 58mm or 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers via Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, LAN, or Sunmi internal printers.
How does Offline Mode and Real-Time Cloud Sync work?+
Transactions write locally in under 15ms. When online, sales automatically sync to your cloud analytics dashboard without interrupting service.
Why subscribe on the web vs. App Store / Google Play?+
Subscribing directly through our Web Portal gives you our best flat-rate pricing ($49/mo Plus, $99/mo Pro) with zero store surcharges.
What happens if a device breaks or crashes?+
FlexiTap performs daily automated cloud backups. Log into a replacement terminal to restore your store menu and layout instantly.
Are there long-term contracts or cancellation fees?+
None. FlexiTap is a transparent monthly or annual SaaS subscription that you can cancel anytime with zero penalties.
