Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hardware, offline mode, and subscriptions.

Can I bring my own tablet and thermal receipt printers?+
Yes! FlexiTap is 100% Bring-Your-Own-Device (BYOD). It runs on standard Android tablets, with iOS (iPads) and Windows touchscreen editions currently in development. Connect to standard 58mm or 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers via Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Network/LAN, or Sunmi handheld internal printers.
How does Offline Mode and Real-Time Cloud Sync work?+
All sales, inventory movements, and shift ledgers write instantly on-device in under 15ms. When connected to Wi-Fi/LTE, changes automatically synchronize to your cloud analytics portal. If your internet connection drops, you can keep ringing up orders and printing receipts without any disruption.
Why subscribe on the web vs. App Store / Google Play?+
Subscribing directly through our Web Customer Portal gives you our best flat-rate pricing ($49/mo Plus, $99/mo Pro) with zero store surcharges. Once subscribed, you can log in and activate your subscription across any connected Android, iPad, or Windows device.
What happens if a device breaks or crashes?+
FlexiTap performs daily automated cloud backups of all business records. With our Safe-Boot Restoration system, you can log into a replacement master register and restore your store layout and menu in minutes.
Are there long-term contracts or cancellation fees?+
None. FlexiTap is a transparent SaaS subscription. You can switch between Monthly and Annual billing or cancel anytime with zero penalties.
