Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about hardware, offline mode, and subscriptions.

Can I use my own tablet and receipt printers?+
Yes! FlexiTap is 100% Bring-Your-Own-Device (BYOD). It runs smoothly on standard Android tablets and phones. Connect to standard 58mm or 80mm ESC/POS thermal printers via Bluetooth, USB OTG, or Network/LAN.
What happens if my Wi-Fi or cellular network drops?+
FlexiTap is built with an offline-first SQLite database. You can ring up sales, take cash, and print customer receipts with zero interruption. The instant connectivity returns, sales summaries automatically sync to the cloud.
How do I access the Web Customer Portal?+
The Live Web Analytics Portal is included with Plus and Pro plans. Log in from any desktop or mobile browser using your store owner credentials to view real-time sales, tax totals, and export CSV/PDF reports.
Are there any long-term contracts or cancellation fees?+
None. FlexiTap is a transparent SaaS subscription. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime directly from your dashboard.
