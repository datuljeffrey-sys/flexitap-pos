🛠️ Diagnostic Prerequisites & Supported Systems

FlexiTap Remote Support is powered by an ultra-lightweight, high-performance diagnostic bridge (RustDesk engine). It allows our technical support team to inspect hardware peripherals, diagnose offline printer queues, and troubleshoot POS settings with zero bloat.

💻 Supported Platforms
Windows 10/11 POS registers, Android handheld terminals, macOS, and Linux workstations.
🖨️ Peripheral Diagnostics
ESC/POS thermal receipt printers (USB & LAN), Bluetooth barcode scanners, and cash drawers.
🔒 Zero-Trust Security & Merchant Isolation

Your business security and financial privacy are strictly protected during every remote support interaction:

  • ✓
    Merchant Permission Required: Support technicians cannot initiate or view your screen without your explicit approval. You must click "Accept" on your POS register.
  • ✓
    End-to-End TLS 1.3 Encryption: All diagnostic traffic is fully encrypted in transit using ChaCha20-Poly1305 and TLS 1.3 protocol standards.
  • ✓
    Isolated Floor Staff Mode: Counter staff and baristas can receive urgent hardware assistance without revealing the store owner's passwords, bank details, or sales analytics.
  • ✓
    Instant One-Click Disconnect: You maintain full control. You can terminate the session at any second, immediately revoking all screen access. Zero persistent background daemons remain.
