How Offline Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) Work Without Internet

Most cloud POS systems drop drink tickets or delay food prep when your coffee shop Wi-Fi disconnects. FlexiTap routes kitchen order tickets directly over your local network in real-time.

Instant KOT Routing: From Counter to Barista Station in Under 300ms

When a customer orders an iced oat latte with an extra shot and a warm almond croissant, your front-of-house cashier punches the ticket. Even if an internet storm completely knocks out your ISP, FlexiTap writes the ticket to your encrypted local database and fires print triggers instantly:

  • 1
    Beverage & Espresso Routing: Drink tickets print immediately at the barista station printer with exact milk, temperature, and sweetness customizations.
  • 2
    Pastry & Kitchen Routing: Food orders route to the kitchen prep counter or thermal receipt cutter with table numbers and guest tags.
  • 3
    Zero Cloud Round-Trip: Tickets never travel to a remote cloud server before printing. No "buffering spinner," no lost tickets, and zero walkouts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Cafe Operators & Baristas

Everything you need to know about setting up FlexiTap in your coffee shop or cafe.

Can I use FlexiTap in my cafe without an internet connection? +
Yes, 100%. FlexiTap is built from the ground up as an offline-first system using a local SQLite database engine. You can ring up sales, calculate change, apply discounts, and print customer receipts and kitchen order tickets completely offline. When an internet connection is available, data seamlessly backs up to the cloud.
How do Kitchen Order Tickets (KOT) print without internet? +
FlexiTap talks directly to your thermal receipt printers (80mm or 58mm) over your local USB connection, Bluetooth, or internal Wi-Fi/Ethernet router. Because the communication happens over your internal Local Area Network (LAN), zero internet connection to external cloud servers is required to route tickets to the barista or kitchen.
Can I set up beverage modifiers like oat milk and extra espresso shots? +
Yes. FlexiTap features an advanced item modifier system designed specifically for coffee shops. You can configure modifier groups such as Milk Choice (Whole, Oat, Almond), Sweetness Level (0%, 25%, 50%, 100%), Temperature (Hot, Iced), and Extra Add-ons. Modifiers can carry price deltas and appear clearly printed on barista tickets.
What hardware do I need to run FlexiTap in my cafe? +
You can run FlexiTap on hardware you already own! It runs smoothly on Windows 10/11 touchscreen terminals, desktop PCs, laptops, Android POS devices, or as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) on iPads and Macs. For printing, it connects to standard ESC/POS thermal printers from brands like Epson, Star Micronics, Sunmi, Xprinter, and generic thermal receipt printers.
Is the Free Community edition really free for my coffee shop? +
Yes! Our Free Community tier is completely free with zero monthly fees and zero expiration dates. It includes unlimited offline sales transactions, item modifiers, receipt printing, and local inventory tracking for a single register counter. If you later decide you want automatic cloud multi-device sync and remote analytics, you can upgrade to Founders Plus or Pro.
