Privacy Policy

VERSION 2.4 — EFFECTIVE NOVEMBER 21, 2026

FlexiTap (“FlexiTap”, “we”, “us”, “our”) provides an offline-first point-of-sale, inventory management, and business operational software platform to commercial businesses worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is processed, who we share it with, and how you can exercise your legal rights. It applies to the FlexiTap mobile and desktop applications, the web portal at https://flexitapapp.com, and any related software services (collectively, the “Service”).

Applicable Privacy Laws & Global Scope:
This Policy is intended to apply globally. Depending on where you, your business, your employees, or your customers are located, additional data protection laws (including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and applicable international privacy frameworks) may apply to personal data processed through the Service. Nothing in this Policy limits rights or obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded under the applicable law of the data subject’s or merchant’s jurisdiction. Where required, FlexiTap complies with applicable legal obligations concerning transparency, security, data-subject rights, breach response, cross-border transfers, and regulatory cooperation.

1. Controller and Processor Roles
For account administration, subscription management, billing records, security monitoring, fraud prevention, diagnostic telemetry, customer support, and legal compliance, FlexiTap acts as an independent data controller (or business under applicable privacy laws). Our Merchant of Record, Paddle, acts as an independent data controller for payment processing, subscription transactions, and statutory tax remittance.

For customer records, employee timecards, supplier details, and other third-party operational information that a merchant records or uploads into the Service, the merchant acts as the data controller, and FlexiTap acts as a data processor (or service provider): we process that data solely on the merchant's behalf and pursuant to documented instructions to provide the Service. The merchant remains responsible for establishing a lawful basis for processing, providing all required privacy notices to individuals, and honoring data subject rights requests under the laws of its operating jurisdiction.
2. What We Collect and Sensitive Data Exclusions

Account data. Business name, owner or administrator name, business email address, physical address, business telephone number, store logo, locale settings, and subscription metadata (such as order IDs and customer tokens issued by Paddle).

Staff data. Employee names, email addresses, phone numbers, role and permission assignments, secure 4-digit login PINs, and shift attendance records created by the merchant.

Customer data recorded by merchants. Names, phone numbers, physical delivery addresses, transaction notes, loyalty point balances, and internal customer store credit / house account ledgers recorded at the merchant's discretion.

Operational and inventory data. Menu items, modifiers, recipes, stock quantities, vendor lists, sales tickets, discounts, cash register drawer balances, and printable receipt templates.

Device and diagnostic telemetry. Device hardware model, operating system and version, app build, unique installation identifiers used for multi-terminal sync pairing, push notification tokens, IP address, and system runtime crash logs. Crash logs are used solely to debug errors and do not intentionally contain business record contents. Product metrics are aggregated or irreversibly pseudonymized.

Communications. Inquiries, feedback, and diagnostic attachments submitted to customer support or developer channels.

Strict Prohibition of Highly Sensitive Data:
The Service is strictly not designed or intended for the collection or storage of highly sensitive personal data, including national or government identification numbers (e.g., SSNs, passport numbers), biometric data, biometric timeclocks, financial account passwords, or full credit/debit card numbers.

Cardholder Data Exclusion (PCI DSS): FlexiTap does not collect, process, or store full Primary Account Numbers (PANs), CVVs, card PINs, or magnetic stripe/chip track data. In-store card payments are processed entirely off-system by external PCI-compliant terminals. Online subscription billing is handled directly by Paddle under PCI DSS Level 1 certification.

Health and Patient Data Exclusion: FlexiTap does not knowingly collect or process Protected Health Information (PHI). The Service is not a certified electronic health record (EHR) system and must not be used to store patient, medical, prescription, or clinical records under HIPAA or international health privacy regulations.
3. How We Use Your Data and Legal Bases
We process personal data only when supported by a valid legal basis:
  • Performance of Contract: To provision your account, maintain peer-to-peer and cloud database synchronization across paired registers, generate transaction summaries, and deliver core point-of-sale functionality;
  • Subscription Billing: Facilitated through Paddle (Merchant of Record) to process renewals, prevent subscription fraud, and remit applicable sales taxes;
  • Customer Support & Technical Standby: To diagnose sync anomalies, investigate bug reports, and provide merchant assistance;
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain system security, detect abusive activity, prevent unauthorized device pairing, and optimize application performance;
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable tax, financial, accounting, and regulatory recordkeeping duties.
We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your operational records for cross-context behavioral advertising, third-party profiling, or automated credit scoring. The Service is 100% ad-free across all tiers.
4. Subprocessors and Data Sharing
We share data only with vetted third-party service providers and subprocessors bound by strict data protection, confidentiality, and security obligations, strictly as necessary to operate the Service:

Supabase Inc. — User authentication, managed PostgreSQL database infrastructure, real-time WebSocket replication, and tenant-isolated encrypted cloud storage buckets for optional merchant database snapshots (.bak);

Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd / Paddle Payments) — Merchant of Record, subscription billing gateway, tax compliance, and fraud screening. Paddle processes customer payment information as an independent data controller;

Google Drive (Google LLC) — Optional client-side database backup integration initiated directly by the merchant via Google OAuth 2.0. Backup archives are uploaded directly to the merchant's personal Google Drive storage; FlexiTap does not store merchant Google credentials on our servers;

Cloudflare Inc. — Edge network routing, SSL/TLS cryptographic provisioning, DDoS mitigation, and web application firewall security;

RustDesk — Optional on-demand remote desktop tooling for FlexiTap Pro diagnostic support sessions, activated strictly upon explicit merchant PIN consent and real-time merchant supervision, with zero unattended or background access.

We may also disclose information where required by law, valid subpoena, court order, or binding governmental demand, or when necessary to protect the vital interests, safety, or legal rights of FlexiTap, our merchants, or the public.
5. Where Your Data Lives and International Transfers
Operational cloud data is stored in high-security data centers located in the United States and global edge nodes provided by our hosting infrastructure. Because our third-party infrastructure operates internationally, personal data may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from jurisdictions outside your home country. Where cross-border data transfers occur from the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or other jurisdictions requiring transfer safeguards, we rely on standard recognized mechanisms, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and robust supplementary technical measures including end-to-end encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and AES-256 encryption at rest.
6. Retention and Deletion Schedule
We retain account and operational data for as long as your merchant account remains active or as needed to provide the Service.

Upon Account Deletion (https://flexitapapp.com/account/delete):
  • Production Databases: Your account profile, store catalog, and cloud-synced transaction records are scheduled for permanent deletion or irreversible anonymization from active production servers within approximately thirty (30) days;
  • Disaster-Recovery Backups: Encrypted system database snapshots (.bak) and disaster-recovery archives persist in private, restricted storage for up to an additional thirty (30) days before being securely overwritten or permanently purged;
  • Legal & Financial Records: Information required for tax reporting, dispute defense, anti-fraud enforcement, or statutory compliance will be retained strictly for the duration mandated by applicable law;
  • Merchant-Exported Backups: Database files exported to your local devices, external hard drives, or personal Google Drive accounts remain under your exclusive custody and control. FlexiTap cannot and does not delete files from your personal storage destinations.
7. Data Loss and Offline-First Local Storage
The Free Community Tier stores all operational data locally on your physical device. FlexiTap does not maintain server-side copies or cloud backups for Free-tier stores; if your device is damaged, lost, stolen, factory-reset, or the app is uninstalled, your local data cannot be recovered. For all tiers, operational data recorded while offline is queued locally and synchronizes upon reconnecting to the internet. Merchants are solely responsible for creating regular offline database backups and exports using the tools provided under Data Management.
8. Security, Cyber Liability Disclaimer, and Incident Response Protocol
We employ industry-standard technical, organizational, and administrative safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration, including TLS encryption in transit, encrypted storage at rest, token-based authentication, database Row-Level Security (RLS) tenant isolation, private storage access controls, and device de-authorization capabilities.

No Absolute Security or Cyber Insurance: No digital transmission or storage architecture can be guaranteed 100% immune from cyber threats or hardware failure. FlexiTap is a software provider and does not carry commercial cyber liability insurance covering merchant operations. You are solely responsible for securing physical devices, enforcing staff PIN confidentiality, maintaining local Wi-Fi security, and obtaining independent commercial insurance if required for your business.

Security Incident Response Protocol:
If FlexiTap confirms a security incident or unauthorized access impacting personal data processed through the Service, FlexiTap will take immediate, reasonable steps to contain, investigate, mitigate, and remediate the incident. Where required by applicable law, FlexiTap will notify the competent supervisory authorities, affected data subjects, and/or the affected merchant without undue delay. Where the merchant acts as data controller and is responsible for notifying its customers or staff, FlexiTap will provide reasonable cooperation and diagnostic information, subject to confidentiality, law enforcement, and security constraints.
9. Data Subject Rights & Requests Concerning Merchant-Submitted Data
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your personal data, or object to certain processing without discriminatory treatment.

Account Holders: You can edit profile information in the app or delete your cloud account at https://flexitapapp.com/account/delete. To submit a data access or portability request, email privacy@flexitapapp.com.

Requests Concerning Merchant-Submitted Data:
If your personal data (such as a customer contact, store credit record, or employee timecard) was collected or submitted to the Service by a merchant or employer, please direct your privacy request first to that merchant organization. The merchant acts as the data controller determining the purposes of processing and is responsible for responding to individual rights requests. FlexiTap will provide reasonable technical assistance to the merchant to fulfill valid statutory requests.
10. Children's Privacy
The Service is intended strictly for commercial business use by adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children in violation of applicable laws (including COPPA). If you believe an unauthorized minor has submitted personal data to FlexiTap, please contact privacy@flexitapapp.com, and we will promptly assess the request and delete the information in accordance with applicable law.
11. Data Processing Addendum (DPA) for Merchants
To the extent FlexiTap processes personal data of customers, employees, or third parties on behalf of a merchant in its capacity as a data processor (or service provider), the following operational terms apply:
  • Documented Instructions: FlexiTap processes such data solely to provide, maintain, and support the Service in accordance with the Terms of Service and documented merchant configuration;
  • Confidentiality: Personnel authorized to access merchant data are bound by formal confidentiality obligations;
  • Security Safeguards: FlexiTap implements and maintains the technical and organizational security measures described in Section 8;
  • Subprocessor Authorization: The merchant grants general authorization for FlexiTap to engage the subprocessors listed in Section 4, subject to equivalent data protection obligations;
  • Assistance with Rights: FlexiTap provides technical capabilities within the software (e.g., export, correction, deletion) enabling the merchant to honor data subject requests;
  • Incident Support: FlexiTap will promptly notify and assist the merchant in the event of a confirmed security incident as set out in Section 8;
  • Deletion or Return: Upon account termination, FlexiTap will delete or return merchant operational data in accordance with the Retention Schedule in Section 6.
12. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect product updates, operational changes, or legal requirements. We will indicate the version and revision date at the top of this document and provide prominent notice in the application or by email for material changes. Continued use of the Service following such updates constitutes acknowledgment of the revised Policy.
