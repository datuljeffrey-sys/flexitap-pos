Privacy Policy

FlexiTap (“FlexiTap”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is an offline-first point-of-sale, inventory management, and business operational software application provided to merchants worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to the FlexiTap mobile and desktop apps, the web portal at https://flexitapapp.com, and any related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Where this Policy refers to “personal data”, we mean any information that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined under applicable data protection laws, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA, Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 / DPA), and other applicable international privacy frameworks.

1. Who is responsible for your data (Controller vs. Processor Roles)
For data we collect directly about you, the account holder (your email, business profile, subscription tier, and device metadata), FlexiTap acts as the data controller (or Personal Information Controller / PIC under Philippine law).

For customer records, employee timecards, or supplier contacts that you record into the Service, you act as the data controller (PIC) and FlexiTap acts strictly as your data processor (or Personal Information Processor / PIP): we process that data solely on your behalf and on your documented instructions to provide the Service. You are solely responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and process such information, for providing any required notices to the individuals concerned, and for honoring their data subject rights under the laws of your operating jurisdiction.
2. What we collect

Account data. Email address, business name and type, business address and contact information, business logo, country and locale, and the subscription receipt, order ID, or customer token issued by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

Staff data. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, role and permission assignments, login PINs, and login activity for staff accounts you create.

Customer data you record. Names, phone numbers, addresses, notes, loyalty point balances and ledger, outstanding debt and debt payment history, and any photos of payment proofs you upload (e.g. transfer slips). You decide what to record.

Transactional and operational data. Items, item variants, modifiers, taxes, discounts, payment methods, transactions, orders, shifts, settlements, stock levels and adjustments, receipts, and printable templates.

Exclusion of Cardholder Data (PCI DSS): FlexiTap explicitly excludes Cardholder Data. We do not collect, process, or store full credit or debit card Primary Account Numbers (PANs), card expiration dates, CVVs, or card magnetic stripe/chip track data. Any card payment processed at merchant locations is handled directly off-system by physical payment terminals or certified third-party merchant acquirers. For web subscription billing, all card details are submitted directly to our Merchant of Record, Paddle, under PCI DSS Level 1 compliance.

Exclusion of Protected Health Information (HIPAA): FlexiTap does not collect or process Protected Health Information (PHI) and is not intended for use with patient medical data.

Device and technical data. Device model, operating system and version, app version, locale, a per-device identifier and device key used for sync and pairing, push-notification token, IP address, and approximate region derived from it.

Crash and diagnostic data. Stack traces, performance metrics, and similar runtime information used to detect and fix bugs. We do not intentionally include the contents of your business records in these reports.

Communications. Messages you send to support and any attachments you provide.
3. How we use your data and the legal bases
  • To provide the Service — keeping your data in sync across your paired devices, processing your transactions, generating receipts, sending operational push notifications, and similar (basis: performance of our contract with you);
  • To process payments and subscriptions — through Paddle (our Merchant of Record), which validates transactions, processes subscriptions, and remits taxes on our behalf (basis: performance of our contract);
  • To provide customer support when you contact us (basis: performance of our contract and our legitimate interest in resolving your inquiry);
  • To improve reliability through aggregated and anonymized crash and performance metrics (basis: our legitimate interest in maintaining a working product);
  • To comply with legal obligations and to protect the rights, safety, or property of FlexiTap, our users, or the public (basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising or profiling. The Service is 100% ad-free across all tiers.
4. Who we share data with
We share data only with service providers we rely on to operate the Service, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform their function. Each is bound by appropriate confidentiality and data-processing obligations.

Supabase – user authentication, PostgreSQL database hosting, realtime synchronization, and private, tenant-isolated cloud storage buckets for encrypted merchant database backups (.bak).

Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) – Merchant of Record, subscription billing, fraud detection, and tax remittance. We never see or store your full payment card details.

Google Drive (Google LLC) – optional manual database snapshot backups initiated by you, stored directly in your own personal Google Drive account via Google OAuth 2.0. We do not store or access your personal Drive files or credentials on our servers.

Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) – edge security, content delivery, SSL certificate provisioning, and DDoS protection for our website and API.

RustDesk (Remote Desktop Tooling) – optional on-demand remote support for Founders Pro merchants, used strictly during authorized diagnostic support sessions with explicit merchant PIN authorization and real-time merchant supervision, without background or unattended access.

Hosting and infrastructure providers we use to operate our backend.

We may also disclose data when required by law, valid legal process, or a binding order from a competent authority, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of FlexiTap, our users, or the public. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, and we will notify you before it becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
5. Where your data lives and international transfers
We store operational data encrypted at rest on servers operated by us and by our hosting providers. Backups are encrypted and retained for up to 30 days. Some of our service providers (including those listed above) operate globally, which means your data may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from countries other than the one in which you reside, including countries that may not provide the same level of data-protection law as your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or equivalent mechanisms.
6. How long we keep your data
We retain your data for as long as your account is active and for as long as we need it to provide the Service. After you delete your account via https://flexitapapp.com/account/delete, we delete or irreversibly anonymize your account and operational data from production systems within a reasonable period (typically 30 days), and remove it from encrypted backups within a further 30 days. We may retain certain information for longer if required to comply with a legal obligation, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.
7. Data loss and Free-tier storage
Free-tier data is stored only on your device. We do not back it up and we cannot recover it if your device is lost, stolen, damaged, factory reset, or if the app is uninstalled. The Service is offline-first: changes you make while offline are queued locally and uploaded the next time the device successfully reaches our servers. If the device is wiped, replaced, or reinstalled before that upload completes, the queued changes are lost. You are solely responsible for maintaining your own backups using the manual export, local database backup, and manual Google Drive backup features available under Data Management in the Service. Please see our Terms of Service for the full allocation of responsibility regarding data loss.
8. Security and cyber liability disclaimer
We employ reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), encrypted database storage at rest, token-based authentication, database Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcing strict tenant isolation, private storage access policies, audit logging, device pairing authorization, and the ability to remotely deactivate paired devices.

No Absolute Security or Cyber Insurance: However, no method of digital storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute immunity from unauthorized access, cyber attacks, hardware theft, or zero-day vulnerabilities. FlexiTap is a startup software provider and does not carry commercial cyber liability insurance covering merchant data or operations. FlexiTap does not act as an insurer of your business data. You are solely responsible for safeguarding your account credentials, enforcing staff PINs, securing your local physical devices and Wi-Fi networks, maintaining your own offline backups, and obtaining independent cyber liability insurance if required for your business operations.
9. Your rights
Depending on your location and the data-protection laws that apply to you, you may have the following rights with respect to personal data we hold about you as an account holder:
  • Access and portability — request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used format. Email support@flexitapapp.com and we will respond within the period required by applicable law (typically 30 days).
  • Correction / rectification — edit your data inside the app, or request corrections.
  • Deletion / erasure — visit https://flexitapapp.com/account/delete to permanently delete your account and all associated operational data.
  • Objection and restriction — object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint — with the competent data-protection supervisory authority in your country of residence.

Jurisdiction-Specific Schedule: Republic of the Philippines (RA 10173)
For individuals and merchants residing or operating within the Republic of the Philippines, you are entitled to all statutory rights under Section 16 of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including the right to be informed, right to access, right to object, right to erasure or blocking, right to rectify, right to damages, and the absolute right to lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines (https://privacy.gov.ph).

Jurisdiction-Specific Schedule: California (CCPA/CPRA) & EU/UK (GDPR)
For users in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and limit the use of sensitive personal information without discrimination. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we process personal data under lawful bases recognized by the GDPR.
10. Children
The Service is intended for use by businesses and is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
11. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Policy from time to time. We will indicate the date of the most recent revision at the top of this page, and where the changes are material we will provide additional notice in the app or by email. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
12. Contact
Questions, requests, or complaints about this Policy or our handling of your data? Contact our Data Privacy Officer at support@flexitapapp.com.
