Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Boutique & Retail Store Owners

Everything you need to know about implementing FlexiTap in your business.

Can I use my existing USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner? +
Yes, 100%. FlexiTap supports any standard HID barcode scanner gun (USB wired, 2.4GHz wireless dongle, or Bluetooth). It instantly detects scans as fast keystrokes, looks up the SKU or UPC locally in SQLite, and carts the product in under 15 milliseconds.
Does retail inventory work when my mall internet cuts out? +
Yes. Unlike cloud-first retail platforms that freeze or throw network error banners, FlexiTap maintains a complete encrypted local database on your checkout terminal. You can scan barcodes, apply discounts, adjust stock, and print receipts without internet. When your connection returns, data syncs seamlessly to your cloud back-office.
Can I import my existing product catalog via CSV or Excel? +
Yes! You can bulk-import tens of thousands of items, variants, SKUs, barcode numbers, cost prices, and retail prices using our standard CSV/Excel import template directly from your back-office portal.
Is the Free Community plan truly free for my retail store? +
Yes! Our Free Community edition has zero monthly subscription fees, zero expiration dates, and zero per-transaction fees from FlexiTap. It supports unlimited sales, full barcode scanning, and local inventory management on a single checkout register.
