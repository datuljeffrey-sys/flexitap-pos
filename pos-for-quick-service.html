How Offline KDS & Bump Bar Broadcast Works Without Internet

Cloud POS vendors drop orders when cell modems drop in food trucks or outdoor events. FlexiTap routes tickets directly over local LAN.

Local Network Broadcast: From Window to Grill in Under 200ms

When 20 customers queue at your food truck window, cloud lag means angry patrons and lost tickets. FlexiTap operates on high-speed local UDP/TCP network sockets that broadcast instantly without internet:

  • 1
    Direct Local Socket Broadcast: Counter terminals broadcast tickets instantly to any tablet or Android TV running the FlexiTap KDS app over local Wi-Fi router.
  • 2
    Color Escalation & Target Timers: Green (0-3 mins), Yellow (3-6 mins), Red (6+ mins). Kitchen staff stay alerted to rush targets with tactile bump-bar key strikes.
  • 3
    100% Offline Food Truck Resilience: Generator power, crowded festivals, and remote pop-ups with zero cell coverage operate with zero disruption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Quick-Service & Food Truck Operators

Everything you need to know about implementing FlexiTap in your business.

Can I run FlexiTap in a food truck with zero cellular signal? +
Yes, 100%! FlexiTap runs completely offline. You can ring orders, calculate change, apply combo meal pricing, and fire tickets to your thermal kitchen printer or tablet KDS over your truck's simple local Wi-Fi router with zero internet or cellular SIM card needed.
What hardware do I need for the Kitchen Display System (KDS)? +
You do not need expensive proprietary restaurant monitors! You can use any consumer Android tablet, iPad, or inexpensive HDMI TV with an Android box. The FlexiTap KDS app connects directly to your counter terminal over your local Wi-Fi network.
How fast is the tap-and-go order speed during lunch rush? +
Orders take under 3 to 5 seconds. With visual category tiles, preset combo buttons, and automated cash drawer kick triggers, cashiers can cycle through high-volume line queues at peak velocity.
Is the Free Community edition really free for my fast-food stall? +
Yes! Our Free Community tier is free forever with zero monthly software fees. It includes unlimited offline sales, combo modifiers, receipt printing, and local inventory tracking for a single checkout station.
