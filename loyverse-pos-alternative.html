Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching from Loyverse POS to FlexiTap.

Why do merchants look for a Loyverse POS alternative? +
While Loyverse advertises a free core app, essential business functions require expensive monthly add-ons: Employee Management is $25/month per store and Advanced Inventory is $25/month per store ($50+/month total). In addition, stores with spotty internet experience sync delays and freezing. FlexiTap eliminates these costs with true offline SQLite resilience and zero mandatory monthly add-on taxes.
Can I keep my existing Loyverse receipt printer? +
Yes, 100%. FlexiTap supports any standard ESC/POS thermal printer (Epson, Star Micronics, Sunmi, Xprinter, etc.) connected via USB, Bluetooth, or Ethernet LAN. Auto-cutters and RJ11 cash drawers trigger out of the box with zero proprietary hardware lock-in.
How difficult is it to migrate my products from Loyverse? +
It takes less than 60 seconds. Simply export your product list CSV from Loyverse Back Office (Items → Export) and upload it to your FlexiTap portal. All your item names, prices, categories, barcodes, and modifiers map automatically.
Does FlexiTap support Kitchen Display Systems (KDS)? +
Yes. FlexiTap includes native real-time KDS kitchen display and Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) printing. Orders routed from cashier registers or floor tablets transmit instantly to your kitchen prep screens over local Wi-Fi or LAN with sub-300ms latency.
Is the FlexiTap Free Tier really free? +
Yes. The FlexiTap Community edition has no trial expiration, no credit card required, and no hidden monthly subscription. You get full cashier register checkout, unlimited transactions, local offline SQLite encryption, and hardware printer integration completely free.
