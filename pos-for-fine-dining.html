How Offline Course Firing Works Without Internet

Dinner rushes cannot pause for cloud buffering. FlexiTap coordinates table mapping and course firing directly over your local network.

Hold & Fire Course Protocol: Precision Kitchen Coordination

Fine dining requires immaculate pacing between appetizers, palate cleansers, mains, and desserts. Cloud POS lags can lead to premature course firing or cold food. FlexiTap manages the entire dinner service cycle locally:

  • 1
    Seat-by-Seat Ordering: Assign appetizers and mains by guest seat number (1-12) so food runners deliver plates without asking "Who had the sea bass?".
  • 2
    Hold & Fire Triggers: Mains remain held in the kitchen queue until the server taps 'Fire Table 14 Mains'. Expo station chits cut immediately over local LAN.
  • 3
    Seamless Bill Splitting: Split evenly among couples, split by specific seat items, or transfer tabs to the bar lounge with zero recalculation errors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Fine Dining & Full-Service Restaurateurs

Everything you need to know about implementing FlexiTap in your business.

Can servers split checks evenly or by seat tableside? +
Yes, easily. Servers can split a bill evenly across any number of cards, split by specific seat numbers, or move specific items to separate tabs (e.g. splitting wine bottles across couples) with zero mathematical confusion.
How does course firing work without an active internet connection? +
FlexiTap communicates directly with your thermal kitchen printers and kitchen display screens over your restaurant's internal Local Area Network (LAN). When a server taps 'Fire Entrees' on their handheld tablet, the print command travels over local Wi-Fi in under 300 milliseconds with zero trip to external cloud servers.
Can we customize our restaurant floor plan with table numbers? +
Yes! You can design custom floor maps representing your exact layout: Main Dining Room, Bar Lounge, Patio, and Private Dining Rooms. Tables show real-time seat counts, server assignments, and visual occupancy colors.
Is the Free Community edition really free for our restaurant? +
Yes! The Free Community plan is completely free forever for single-register setups with zero monthly fees, zero commission fees, and zero expiration dates. You get unlimited offline table ordering, course firing, and thermal receipt printing.
